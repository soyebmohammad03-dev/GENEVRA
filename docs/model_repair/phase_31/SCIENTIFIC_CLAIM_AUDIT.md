## Comparative scope

The nearest comparison is by task and assumptions. Tudball and colleagues constrain inference under selection bias, with regression sensitivity tooling. R survey provides design-based estimation and calibration given design information. Learn then Test controls a predictive risk using an independent calibration setting under its stated conditions. GENEVRA combines a planted common omission, explicit caps and tariffs, separate target coverage and certificate denominators, negative allocation results and a raw-count reference verifier. The distinction is the integrated controlled artifact. Partial identification, variance allocation, bounded-difference intervals and binomial inversion are established. The current record supports no empirical or performance superiority.
