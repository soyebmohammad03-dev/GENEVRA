# Conditional manuscript foundation

**Working title.** When independent samples share a blind spot: coverage bounds and allocation trade-offs in a synthetic certification benchmark.

**Working abstract.** Undercoverage can invalidate a calibration certificate even when independent streams agree. We implement a coverage envelope that propagates externally specified limits on jointly unreachable mass and hidden tail risk into a confidence interval for a bounded mixture mean. In a frozen synthetic benchmark, valid restrictions restored mean coverage in a large-omission case while preventing precise certification. Under a small omission at equal stipulated cost, a worst-case width allocation reduced covered precise intervals from 450/512 to 10/512. Invalid restrictions defeated the envelope. These results characterize a particular synthetic workflow. Empirical availability and validity of the external restrictions remain untested.
