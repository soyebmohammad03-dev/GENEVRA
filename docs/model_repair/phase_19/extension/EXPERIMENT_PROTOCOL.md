# Frozen coverage-envelope benchmark v1

Can externally defensible bounds on the jointly unreachable population turn a shared-blind certification failure into a safe and useful interval, and does planning to minimize a conservative uncertainty proxy improve usable precision at the same charged cost? This is a synthetic fixed-budget test of an integration of established methods. It does not establish that suitable external bounds exist in GENEVRA.

The target is expected paired score mu=.30+234.2 p under fixed, externally supplied conditional means c=.30 and t=234.50. There is no residual category in this synthetic submodel. A reachable population has two strata of known conditional weights .9/.1 and Bernoulli tail probabilities r0/r1. Target tail mass is (1−m)(.9r0+.1r1)+mz, with unseen mass m and unseen conditional tail probability z. External declarations state m in [0,cap] and z in [0,hidden_cap]. Cases and seeds are fixed in config.json. The .60 label cutoff, .60 precision width, and .30 tolerance for error from the fixed anchor p=.004 are retained. Truth and validity of supplied declarations are used only for scoring. A signed hash is not external evidence for those declarations.

Compare equal-spend reachable-only (no missingness correction), equal-spend coverage envelope, planned coverage envelope, planned coverage envelope with hidden-support declaration removed (z≤1), always abstain, and the ideal exact reachable-rate identification interval as a descriptive oracle width only. No procedure samples missing outcomes. Equal-spend and planned policies choose designs before observation from the same accessible channels and budget at tariffs 1 and 2. Planned allocation minimizes the Hoeffding width proxy using independent counts and exact expenditure. It does not optimize the realized KL interval. Allocate whole duplicate blocks and charge every row. Within each stratum, policies use shared prefixes of a latent IID sequence, implemented as independent binomial counts of the shorter prefix and extension. No duplicate contributes an extra unit. Across case, budget, stratum and replicate RNG draws are independent by design. Whole replicate is the uncertainty unit, not an evolutionary founder.

The reachable-rate interval uses binomial KL Chernoff inversion at alpha=.05 with a Bonferroni split across the two sampled strata. The missing-mass hull takes all endpoint combinations before affine score conversion. Report descriptive pointwise Wilson intervals for coverage, correct decisive coverage, precision, coverage with precision, abstention, certificate and false-certificate proportions. Retain counts, widths, and cost. Certificate requires the complete interval to lie within .30 of the declared anchor score. Threshold calling is separate. Abstain-only uses the full physical support [-240,240] and purchases no observations as a policy. Charge its acquisition zero and distinguish it from equal-budget estimator comparisons. Oracle receives no performance claims.

Primary contrast is planned minus equal coverage-envelope precision-with-coverage probability at 32768 cost units in small_omission. Use paired fixed-sample Hoeffding 95% bounds on replicate differences in [-1,1]. Evidence of practical improvement requires the lower limit to exceed .05. Other case and budget outcomes and full-hidden-support ablation are descriptive, without secondary significance tests or pooling across cases. Explicitly expose bad-cap and bad-support failures. No exclusions, optional stopping, parameter changes, or reclassification. 512 replicates per case and budget give worst-case binomial Monte Carlo SE .0221 and a conservative primary paired half-width .1201. Stop and preserve inputs on malformed data or acquisition failure. One worker, CPU only, target under 60 seconds and 512 MiB. No new GENEVRA lifetimes or biological experiments.
