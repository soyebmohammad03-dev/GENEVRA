# Prospective validation protocol v0.2

**Draft for independent review and later registration. No fieldwork or partner authorization.**

## Question, population and roles

At identical purchased information and cost, can the GENEVRA combined policy deliver more precise and correct anchor certificates than direct calibration while keeping adverse certification below a prespecified limit against an independently enumerated target?

The proposed setting is a regional perennial-stream monitoring program. One independent policy unit is a deployment episode in a distinct watershed-season. The within-episode target is the finite roster of eligible reach-weeks under frozen boundaries, reach segmentation and visit windows. Weight every eligible reach-week equally, distinct from EPA's stream-mile estimand. The response is a binary classification under one registered laboratory assay and threshold for a specified analyte, without claiming latent ecological truth. Region, partner, threshold, tolerance delta, useful width w, safety permissions and site inventory remain unavailable.

Channels A and B are proposed operational monitoring rosters with independent collection workflows. Their records may omit the same places. Availability means actually recruitable under the same frozen access rule. Existing convenience records can establish reachability and nominate an anchor, but do not become probability samples by weighting. Each episode purchases a common, documented calibration package with known within-frame inclusion probabilities. An independently governed reference sample is drawn from the full custodian roster, regardless of A/B status. A separate development set freezes assay QC, estimators and losses. No evaluation outcomes tune the policy.

## Reference frame and operational independence

1. **Enumerate first.** A custodian with no responsibility for A/B acquisition constructs reach-week keys from an independent hydrographic layer, field/imagery checks and a registered hydrology rule. Map version, reach breaks, seasons and eligibility are locked before receiving either roster. Mask channel labels during the initial enumeration.
2. **Audit the reference itself.** Use a separately partitioned area frame that covers the registered geography, including hard-to-access areas, with probability-selected segments and known inclusion. An independent mapper checks missing/extra reaches, duplicates and misclassification under the same eligibility rule. Log audit inclusion and deviations. A valid bound U must cover the fraction of the true eligible target missing from the custodian roster, using independently justified counts, not net discrepancy or mapper agreement. Allocate the audit's uncertainty within the reference family. Assign each eligible reach-week uniquely to its segment by a frozen geometric rule. Use the area design to estimate both the count absent from the custodian roster and the total eligible count. With simultaneous upper and positive lower limits respectively, their ratio (capped at one) supplies U. Unvisited segments require defensible count bounds or a full-range fallback. Freeze the count-estimation design with the custodian before selecting sites. If no defensible area frame or bound exists, the study can identify only its enumerated-frame target. For a full-target claim use full support or stop.
3. **Resolve and freeze.** Resolve duplicate keys with geometry, week and registered crosswalk rules. Keep both originals, decision and reason. Blinded second review resolves contested eligibility. A third adjudicator breaks disagreements, with unresolved status retained. Freeze the eligible counts and all exclusions before outcomes. Off-roster finds after lock are recorded as deviations, not silently added to the original denominator.
4. **Link after freeze.** Match each channel independently and log adjudicated inclusion, recruitability and uncertainty. Tabulate the four A/B availability strata, especially neither-channel units. If status is unknown, retain it in the roster and use a separate uncertainty range or refuse the coverage claim.
5. **Sample and measure separately.** Reference stratified SRS without replacement has n_h/N_h inclusion within each roster stratum and N_h/n_h expansion weights. Sample even the neither-channel stratum. Record inaccessible, unsafe, failed and missing visits without replacement. Teams do not see decisions or operational counts. Reference lab personnel see blinded sample IDs and use prespecified calibration, split samples, replicates, blank checks and chain of custody. Borderline or discordant results follow frozen retest rules. Unresolved remains unresolved.
6. **Seal.** The custodian publishes timestamped hashes of roster, sampling protocol, random seed, selected identifiers in escrow and disposition ledger. Acquire policies before releasing reference outcomes. Separate role-specific data and log every access, linkage and amendment. Prefer independent draws with overlap allowed if independently measured and blinded. Any overlap creates unit-level association; score paired policy differences at episode level. A complement draw is dependent and requires explicit design review, not a claim of independent draws.

An off-channel unit discovered by the independent frame establishes a gap in A/B access. Its design-weighted reference outcome may reveal a difference in event rate. Agreement between A and B can neither discover a shared omission nor identify its outcome distribution. Estimating the full target, calibrating on acquired observations, and certifying an anchor are separate steps. An unknown reference-frame gap is another identification problem.

## Reference inference and endpoints

Let theta_j be the finite target proportion in episode j. For each known fixed-size SRSWOR stratum, invert both hypergeometric tails for the positive count. Hull all admissible completions from observed positive count k to k+u unresolved; monotonicity makes the extreme completions sufficient. Weight by roster stratum sizes and widen for independently bounded off-roster mass using hidden outcomes in [0,1]. A zero-draw stratum contributes its full range. The Horvitz–Thompson total divided by roster size is a complete-label design point estimate. No substitution of IID intervals for clustered samples. The software assumes the supplied off-frame cap is valid. If estimated, use a documented split within the .01 family error for cap estimation and outcome bands. Apply a Bonferroni allocation across registered episodes and strata. If measurement errors can produce resolved but incorrect labels, independently bound them and widen the set or stop. Retaining unresolved labels alone does not address misclassification.

For each episode, precommit the acquisition-only anchor a and the rule issuing a certificate of |theta_j−a|≤delta. The latent false certificate is an issued decision with discrepancy greater than delta. Compare the frozen reference set R with [a−delta,a+delta]. R entirely inside means adjudicated correct; disjoint means adjudicated false; overlap without containment means unresolved. Treat interval endpoints as included. **Primary safety** is the unconditional probability of an issued false or unresolved certificate, using the observed adverse indicator as a conservative upper proxy for latent false risk on the joint reference event. **Primary comparative estimand** is the expected paired gain in correct certificates with reported interval width ≤w. Use definite candidate correctness minus possible direct correctness for a conservative lower proxy. Nonissues contribute zero. Do not score only issued decisions.

Mean coverage concerns whether a policy interval contains theta_j; with an uncertain reference report definite coverage if it contains R and possible coverage if it intersects R. Precision is interval width at most w. Coverage with precision, decisive calls, width, bias when identifiable, issuance, abstention, risk among issued, unresolved rate and subgroup outcomes are secondary. Report actual denominators and the conditional/unconditional distinction. Do not interpret full-support coverage as useful precision.

## Fair policy comparison

- **Primary representative access:** GENEVRA combined versus direct design-based calibration. Every method gets the same purchased target-probability calibration sample, channel data, auxiliary restrictions, metadata and anchor construction rule. The combined candidate applies the frozen provenance/disagreement gate and coverage envelope, with full-range fallback. Define the gate and estimator executable from those inputs before registration. Direct uses valid design-weighted intervals from the same target calibration. No access to evaluation measurements for either.
- **Secondary:** stratified direct using shared strata; conservative partial identification using identical mass evidence and unrestricted hidden support; always abstain; and restricted reachable-only transfer as a labeled assumption-violation control. Run the restricted access regime separately. An assumption or source offered to one eligible comparator must be offered to all. If the two policies reduce to the same rule, report equivalence of implementation rather than inventing a difference.
- Match on the same frozen package digest and exact charged cost. Record procurement, travel, attempts, access permits, labor, lab, compute, registry and reference work. Share the independent evaluation cost across methods. Standalone abstention has zero acquisition cost; as a paired policy it does not make the already purchased package cheaper. Allocation changes require separate randomized purchases or preplanned nested designs. Differences in information, billing or policy availability block a primary comparison.

Use two one-sided gates with .02 each and joint reference-family allocation .01. Safety upper ≤.05, and useful gain lower >.10. Fixed-look exact binomial safety and paired Hoeffding lower bound require independent identically sampled episodes, not repeated reaches, visits or overlapping watersheds. On the joint reference event the conservative proxies bound latent false risk and usefulness. Use secondary estimates descriptively unless independently preregistered with multiplicity. A definite unsafe or inferior result is evidence against the operating claim; imprecision can leave it unresolved. Safety through abstention alone cannot pass the gain gate.
