# GENEVRA external statistical review checklist

This checklist is prepared by the project. No external reviewer has answered it.

- Reproduce from a fresh checkout. Report exact commit, runtime, package versions, numerical discrepancies, conflicts and any undocumented steps.
- Independently reconstruct the primary paired numerator and the 17-member secondary adjustment. Check the unit of inference, Hoeffding range, clipping, independence assumptions and whether alternative intervals would materially change interpretation.
- Challenge the estimand. Does covered-and-precise probability measure useful target inference here? Compare it with certification utility, all-run false certification, conditional risk and the width distribution.
- Examine the allocation math separately from coverage. Check continuous optima, integer feasibility, tariffs for duplicate blocks, the paid pilot and its removal from inference. Assess whether equal spend or variance optimization is the appropriate reference. Request a separate design if fixed rules can skip the pilot.
- Audit information access. Identify who knows weights, missing mass, hidden support and true variances. Ask what independent evidence validates the caps. Separate the oracle and refusal control.
- Examine the adversarial examples, including wrong caps and weights. Identify which failures require both shared omission and inadequate external information. Explain which can plausibly generalize, and which depend on the chosen laws, costs or width cutoff.
- Challenge novelty against the closest existing benchmark and field designs. Which parts are established? Is the combined artifact independently useful?
- Specify minimum evidence for an independent holdout. Require policy locks before unblinding, unselected reporting, an independent custodian and a prospective target and loss definition. Empirical safety also needs a separately audited frame and approved reference measurement.

Record each item as supported, contradicted or unresolved, with evidence. Internal tests and this checklist provide no external endorsement.
