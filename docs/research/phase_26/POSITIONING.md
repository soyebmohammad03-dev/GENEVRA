# GENEVRA research positioning

## Contribution decision

| Route | Foundations and evidence | Novelty and missing validation | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. New allocation strategy | A width proxy with a correct integer optimizer. The primary case favors cost-adjusted Neyman and equal spend | Objective is elementary and the tested rule underperformed. Generalized or optimized allocation would require a newly registered study | Reject as the current contribution. Preserve the negative result as a comparator |
| B. Reproducible benchmark of certification and allocation under shared undercoverage | Known-truth failures, frozen access regimes, paid-pilot comparisons, valid-cap and invalid-cap cases, reproducible public capsules | Originality is limited to the particular experimental combination and implementation. External artifact reuse, nearest-benchmark comparison and withheld cases remain missing | Primary benchmark and systems route, with submission on hold |
| C. Diagnostic and verification framework | Separates coverage, width, certification, utility and access. Uses independently written numerical and aggregation checks | Separate endpoints and uncertainty checks are established. The auditable workflow supports B, and its independent usability remains untested | Supporting contribution. A narrow negative feasibility technical report is the fallback |

No new estimator or theory is established. The potentially distinct contribution is a specified, replayable comparison that traces both outcome and failure attribution to cost, access, assumptions and replicate records. Independent technical review could judge reuse and relevance. Empirical validation would require an independently governed target frame, measurements, cost evidence and approval. A separate held-out synthetic evaluation would test transfer across benchmark cases while leaving the empirical question open.

## Established foundations and GENEVRA choices

| Bibliographic record | Boundary for GENEVRA |
|---|---|
| Jerzy Neyman (1934), *On the Two Different Aspects of the Representative Method: The Method of Stratified Sampling and the Method of Purposive Selection*. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society 97(4), 558–625. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2397-2335.1934.tb04184.x) | Variance-optimal stratification is established. The two-stratum paid-pilot implementation and smoothing rule are choices. |
| Tommy Wright (2019), *Direct Proof of Exact Sample Allocation Optimality with Cost Constraints*. U.S. Census Bureau, Research Report RRS2019-03. [Report](https://www.census.gov/library/working-papers/2019/adrm/RRS2019-03.html) | Exact integer allocation with cost constraints is established. The benchmark compares realized width with a separate objective. |
| Pierre Étoré and Benjamin Jourdain (2010), *Adaptive Optimal Allocation in Stratified Sampling Methods*. Methodology and Computing in Applied Probability 12(3), 335–360. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11009-008-9108-0) | Variance learning during acquisition is established. One pilot with independent inference is a limited two-stage design. |
| D. G. Horvitz and D. J. Thompson (1952), *A Generalization of Sampling Without Replacement From a Finite Universe*. Journal of the American Statistical Association 47(260), 663–685. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1952.10483446) | Known positive inclusion supports design-based target estimation. Weighting cannot create coverage of inaccessible units. |
| Sharon L. Lohr and J. N. K. Rao (2006), *Estimation in Multiple-Frame Surveys*. Journal of the American Statistical Association 101(475), 1019–1030. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000000195) | Independent probability samples and frame overlap can improve estimation. Shared gaps require additional frame evidence. |
| Donald B. Rubin (1976), *Inference and Missing Data*. Biometrika 63(3), 581–592. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/63.3.581) | Ignorability is conditional on assumptions about missingness. Agreement of reachable channels does not establish them. |
| Charles F. Manski (2005), *Partial Identification with Missing Data: Concepts and Findings*. International Journal of Approximate Reasoning 39(2–3), 151–165. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijar.2004.10.006) | Bounded missing-data identification is established. The affine width floor and invalid-cap controls instantiate it. |
| Matthew J. Tudball, Rachael A. Hughes, Kate Tilling, Jack Bowden and Qingyuan Zhao (2023), *Sample-constrained Partial Identification with Application to Selection Bias*. Biometrika 110(2), 485–498. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/asac042) | Estimated constraints have their own uncertainty. GENEVRA's supplied caps are exogenous, with no guarantee that field estimates would be equally informative. |
| Wassily Hoeffding (1963), *Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables*. Journal of the American Statistical Association 58(301), 13–30. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830) | The paired contrast interval uses established bounded-difference concentration for independent replicate differences. It is often conservative. |
| Yonatan Geifman and Ran El-Yaniv (2017), *Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks*. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30. [Proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html) | Selective risk and issuance are established distinct outcomes. GENEVRA applies this distinction to synthetic population-anchor certificates. |
| Anastasios N. Angelopoulos, Stephen Bates, Emmanuel J. Candès, Michael I. Jordan and Lihua Lei (2025), *Learn then Test: Calibrating Predictive Algorithms to Achieve Risk Control*. Annals of Applied Statistics 19(2), 1641–1662. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/24-AOAS1998) | Finite-sample calibration and multiplicity are established. GENEVRA does not fit this framework or acquire representative field calibration data. |

A field precedent for independent spatial coverage auditing is the Norwegian fisheries study cited with its full bibliographic record in the [Phase 23 literature audit](../phase_23/LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY.md). The [Phase 25 comparator review](../phase_25/COMPARATORS.md) lists further costs and scope assumptions. These comparisons are focused, rather than an exhaustive novelty search.
