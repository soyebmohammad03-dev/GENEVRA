# Matched-information and cost comparison

This comparison is proposed. No policy was evaluated on field outcomes in Phase 21.

## Access regimes

- **Restricted.** Every policy receives the same probability acquisition within the operationally reachable subframe, the same documented target population definition, and the same independent coverage information with uncertainty. With a valid frame, the direct baseline estimates the reachable mean and its naive transfer is reported as an assumption-violation stress policy. No policy sees outcomes outside that frame unless that purchase is included for all.
- **Representative calibration available.** Every policy receives the same purchased full-target probability calibration data and the same auxiliary evidence. A direct full-target design-based interval is then a valid comparator. This is a separate regime with a different price and assumption set. Do not rank restricted and representative methods as equal access.
- **Independent reference.** This is an evaluation cost shared by every policy, and its outcomes remain sequestered until decisions are frozen. A calibration/reference split may consume scarce sites. Treat the external access, nonresponse follow-up, linkage and lab work as actual charged resources.

Matched means a fixed purchased acquisition package within each episode. Log frame maintenance, recruitment, unsuccessful visits, travel, equipment, lab assays, computation, and adjudication time separately. Charge real staff time and money at predeclared rates and record who pays. Study-wide setup and per-episode marginal costs are reported separately. Abstention uses the shared package in the policy comparison and costs zero incremental data acquisition if chosen before procurement. The study must distinguish these counterfactuals. Comparing acquisition allocations requires a separate randomized purchasing comparison or a prospectively defined common sample with cost-accounted subdesigns. The shared package alone cannot demonstrate procurement savings.

## Baselines

| Policy | Minimum inputs and eligibility |
|---|---|
| Direct design-based calibration | Same package and probability design. Target-valid in representative access. Restricted direct reports the reachable estimand and nominal transfer is labeled an adverse control. |
| Stratified direct | Same raw data and strata. Uses design weights for the target actually covered. No renormalization over omitted target strata. |
| Conservative partial identification | Same reachable confidence set, full [0,1] hidden support and common independent mass bound. Without a valid mass cap, use full support. |
| GENEVRA omission-aware envelope | Same package, mass bound and defensible hidden support available to every comparator. Score the incremental decision effect against the primary direct policy. No claim of a new statistical method if an equivalent comparator agrees. |
| Abstain-only | Always refuses and returns the full identified range. Report its cost as above. |
| Other methods | Aronow–Lee bounded selection only with justified probability limits and comparable estimand. Multiple-frame design estimators only with valid union coverage and overlap. Learn then Test only with independent calibration episodes, shared loss and equivalent access. Weighted conformal is not a primary comparator for population-mean confidence. |

## Prespecified endpoints and falsification

Use the representative regime for primary paired superiority over direct full-target calibration. Restricted results characterize safety and refusal under limited access, with planned adverse controls reported separately. Primary gate requires **both** an upper 98% bound of at most .05 on unconditional adverse certificate risk and a lower 98% bound greater than .10 on useful correct-certificate gain over direct at the same cost. Reference confidence uses a separate .01 family error. Failure of either gate blocks the comparative claim. A confidence interval crossing the gate is inconclusive about the true value. Conclusive performance below the margin or above the safety limit is evidence against the proposed operating target under that design.

Secondary endpoints are overall and decisive mean coverage, widths, bias where reference supports it, admission, threshold errors, certification yield, abstention, errors conditional on issuance, unresolved reference fraction, subgroup coverage and cost components. Use explicitly exploratory intervals unless a multiplicity plan is independently preregistered. Report worst-case unresolved and agreed reference assumptions separately. Held-out outcomes may neither pick a policy nor change the cost, thresholds or endpoints. If comparable access or outcome adjudication fails, stop the prospective comparison and preserve all observations.
