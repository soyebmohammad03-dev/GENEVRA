# Prospective external validation protocol v0.1

**Status: proposed and unexecuted.** The Census diagnostic is a separate completed exercise. No partner, site, outcome threshold, acquisition price or target roster has been obtained. This protocol freezes the scientific rules for a design review. A later timestamped registration must fill the operational parameters before any outcomes are acquired or examined. The design uses a non-biological monitoring endpoint and cannot establish GENEVRA's evolvability or organismal validity.

## Target and independent custody

Proposed domain is regional stream monitoring, informed by EPA's probability-survey design. Define one deployment episode as a nonoverlapping watershed-season with a finite list of eligible reach-week units. Restrict the scope to perennial reaches in the participating region and a fixed predeclared sampling window, with a registered reach segmentation and week rule. The target within each episode is the **unit-weighted fraction of eligible reach-weeks exceeding a predeclared laboratory assay threshold**. This differs from EPA's stream-mile estimand. Choose the analyte and threshold with a prospective partner based on the monitoring question. They are study parameters, not a regulatory declaration. Set minimum practical certificate tolerance delta, width w and any threshold-call boundary on the probability scale before unblinding. No import of synthetic score cutoffs.

An independent custodian starts with an externally maintained hydrographic frame and verifies a probability sample of reach status, off-roster edges and access barriers before outcomes. Freeze the roster, unit IDs, size, eligibility and crosswalk to existing operational sources. Mask from acquisition teams the identities of evaluation selections. Record why each operational channel can or cannot access each unit. Reachable is the union of eligible, actually recruitable units in those channels, determined by the preregistered rule. Separate RNG seeds are insufficient. If the independent roster cannot reasonably cover difficult reaches, restrict the target transparently before outcomes or stop. No generalization outside the registered frame.

## Acquisition, sampling and adjudication

Use three data roles with disjoint selected unit IDs where within-episode partition is feasible, and independent randomization. The evaluator samples from the full custodian frame irrespective of operational reachability. A clean implementation draws the reference sample first, holds its identifiers in escrow, then samples acquisition from the complement using the corresponding design. Acquisition teams receive only authorized inputs. A reference draw plus complement draw creates dependence across roles. The paired episode policy analysis retains that dependence, and the reference bounds target the original finite roster under the *marginal* reference design. If policy evaluation or bounds require independence from calibration conditional on acquired outcomes, redesign with independent probabilistic draws and a preregistered overlap treatment or separate held-out episodes. Never assert that disjoint IDs imply independent randomization.

1. **Development.** Separate episodes for instrument agreement, eligibility audits, cost feasibility and freezing any models or allocation. No outcomes from evaluation episodes tune the method.
2. **Acquisition.** A shared charged package from probability-selected reachable units. If a target-representative calibration channel is purchased, all methods see the same data. Convenience monitoring data may define operational reachability. They cannot be treated as a probability sample. Custodian supplies a coverage estimate or deterministic bound from a separate roster audit with recorded uncertainty. Freeze any hidden-outcome restriction and justification, or use [0,1].
3. **Reference.** Stratified simple random sampling without replacement across the full roster, including both reachable and unreachable units. If h has size N_h and draws n_h>0, inclusion is n_h/N_h and design weights are N_h/n_h. Stratify by reachability and a predeclared geographic factor only if sample sizes support it. Do not replace unavailable sites post hoc. Blinded field teams collect split samples using the same visit window and protocol. An independent laboratory uses a frozen assay, duplicate QC and blinded adjudication. Outcome is the registered laboratory classification, with an unresolved category for QC failures, discordance or insufficient precision. It is not assumed to be latent environmental truth.

Eligibility excludes only units outside the predeclared frame, season and reach rule, or duplicate IDs resolved before selection. Eligible access denials, unsafe visits, missing samples, protocol deviations and failed QC stay in the denominator with reason codes. No outcome-dependent exclusion. Preserve attempt logs and raw adjudication records under the partner's release terms. Compare response by design stratum. Treat unresolved outcomes as [0,1] in the primary analysis. Model-based missingness adjustments are sensitivity analyses.

## Target reference and error control

Use a stratified finite-population confidence set for the reference target. For each fixed-size SRSWOR stratum, invert hypergeometric tails for the total number of positives. If k labels are positive and u unresolved, hull the compatible intervals from k through k+u. Weight stratum limits by N_h/N. The central design estimate when labels are complete is the Horvitz–Thompson total divided by N. Allocate **.01 family error** for all evaluation-reference bands across episodes and strata. If unknown inclusion or zero probability persists, replace unsupported components by their full feasible range or stop. Any claimed confidence interval for acquisition must incorporate the uncertainty of m, z and reference-quality inputs and the sampling design. The current IID Bernoulli simulator routines must not be applied directly to clustered or SRSWOR field samples.

Freeze an acquisition-only anchor a_j and a certificate rule per episode j. A certificate asserts |theta_j−a_j|≤delta. Using the held-out reference band, classify issued certificates as adjudicated correct if the band is contained in [a_j−delta,a_j+delta], false if disjoint, and unresolved otherwise. Track every certificate. For primary safety count false **or unresolved** issued certificates as adverse. Nonissues are zero in that unconditional event. Also report risk among issued with its actual denominator. For comparative usefulness, score a correct certificate that meets width w. Use the GENEVRA definitely-correct indicator and direct calibration possibly-correct indicator to conservatively bound their paired difference. The common simultaneous reference event supports these inequalities. Insufficient reference precision therefore costs both safety and useful decision yield rather than manufacturing labels.

## Pilot, power and stop

The smallest proposed step is a **12-episode feasibility pilot**, contingent on one partner, a credible custodian, and documented access to units outside both operational rosters. It tests frame reconciliation, field timing, adjudication and cost accounting. Before pilot observations, register the region, endpoint, tolerance, sampling counts, cost ceiling and accepted reference precision. Stop if any target stratum lacks a plausible positive evaluation probability, if the laboratory cannot deliver the registered precision or if access or data terms cannot be reconciled. No confirmatory risk or superiority claims from the pilot.

Future confirmation uses one candidate policy and one comparator, with one-sided .02 allocated to safety and .02 to comparative usefulness plus the .01 simultaneous reference family. Assuming independent, identically sampled deployment episodes, use a one-sided exact binomial upper bound for unconditional adverse risk and a one-sided paired Hoeffding lower bound for usefulness on episode differences in [-1,1]. Independence must be justified from deployment sampling, clustering and governance. If episodes are sampled from a finite roster without replacement, or watersheds/crews are reused, specify a finite-population or cluster-based analysis and power before collection. Sites, visits and repeated method decisions do not substitute for independent episodes.

Illustrations for the IID design, using the declared significance allocations: zero adverse events require at least 77 episodes for an exact upper bound at most 5%. At a true adverse rate of 1%, n=209 with a passing count at most four gives about 94% binomial power. A paired gain of 20 percentage points versus a required 10-point margin needs n≥783 just for that observed difference to clear the Hoeffding lower bound. A sufficient n for 90% power by the same bound under a true 20-point gain is 2,444, before unresolved reference penalties. These are planning calculations, not recruitment targets or achieved power. Feasibility should estimate adjudication rates and practical effect sizes before choosing a confirmatory sample size. No optional stopping or sample-size extension based on favorable outcomes.
