# External dataset assessment

## Search and decision rule

On 2026-11-08, searched agency releases, archive records, and primary papers using combinations of “post-enumeration survey independent coverage”, “demographic analysis coverage error age series”, “probability sample nitrate site weights”, “survey nonresponse public use”, “partial identification selection bias replication”, “distribution shift public benchmark”, and “independent fitness repeatability”. Followed original sources and access terms. Screened candidates for a defined target and eligible units, separate frame construction, linked and independently measured outcomes, identifiable sampling probabilities, sufficient independent policy episodes, costs, and redistributable access. This is a structured feasibility search, without an exhaustive systematic-review claim.

No screened source satisfies all criteria for a valid, matched-cost test of the GENEVRA certificate. The two Census releases were used only for the documented aggregate diagnostic. Restricted records, large unrelated data and unverifiable inputs were not downloaded. Public availability and suitability are separate judgments.

| U.S. Census Bureau, *National Demographic Analysis Tables: 2020*, March 2022 Table 3, and *Census Bureau Releases Experimental Estimates of State and County Undercounts and Overcounts of Young Children*, April 2024. Federal agency work generally lacks U.S. copyright protection. Cite the agency and retain release attribution. [Policy](https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf) | April 1, 2020 U.S. resident population, ages 0–4 in this diagnostic. Administrative demographic accounting from births, deaths and migration rather than a probability sample of Census respondents. Three 2022 model series and a separate 2024 national count pair. No true matched child-level outcome in the public files. | Directly useful to show reference-assumption sensitivity. Net error combines omissions and overcounts. Aggregate estimates have neither linked unit outcomes nor costed access or independent policy episodes. **Diagnostic used, validation ineligible.** |
| U.S. Census Bureau, *National Census Coverage Estimates for People in the United States by Demographic Characteristics*, 2022 PES. [Report and tables](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/coverage-measurement/pes/national-census-coverage-estimates-by-demographic-characteristics.pdf) · [Design comparison](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/blogs/random-samplings/2022/03/upcoming-2020-census-coverage-estimates.html) | Public aggregate release. Same Census attribution policy. No confidential linkage files obtained. | Probability sample of blocks and matching/reconciliation with Census records. PES excludes group quarters and remote Alaska. Estimates and standard errors, not complete true counts. | Good precedent for separate collection and coverage assessment. Matching and dependence assumptions remain. The public age table cannot be treated as another draw from the DA universe or used for GENEVRA performance. **Context only.** |
| EPA, *National Rivers and Streams Assessment 2018–2019 Technical Support Document* and associated 2018–19 files. [Technical document](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-12/nrsa-2018-19-tsd-final-11252024.pdf) · [Versioned report dataset, DOI 10.23719/1531934](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/national-rivers-and-streams-assessment-2018-2019-date-files-for-report-national-rivers-and) · [Data and attribution guidance](https://www.epa.gov/national-aquatic-resource-surveys/frequent-questions-about-data-national-aquatic-resource-surveys) | Public agency survey files. Attribute EPA and survey partners per guidance. No record-level redistribution proposed. | Probability sample of perennial river and stream miles across the contiguous U.S., with standardized site visits, weights and laboratory outcomes. Technical document reports 1,808 probability sites. This is a measured sample, not a census or an error-free reference. | Best candidate for a future domain-specific design. Public files do not by themselves establish a second independently sampled, time-matched reference frame or comparable operational costs. Screening does not claim a working match to Water Quality Portal. **Protocol analogue and potential partner route.** |
| CDC/NCHS, *NHANES 2017–2018*, sample design and nonresponse report. [Original files](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/continuousnhanes/default.aspx?BeginYear=2017) · [Technical report](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/data/nhanes/analyticguidelines/17-18-sampling-variability-nonresponse-508.pdf) | Public-use under the [NCHS data user agreement](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html). True PSUs and restricted linkage are not public. | Stratified multistage sample of civilian noninstitutionalized people. Respondent interviews, exams and laboratory measurements, survey weights and masked variance units. Published analysis reports 16,211 eligible people in this cycle. | Supports weight-aware analysis of measured respondents. Outcomes of nonrespondents and an independent linked verification sample are unavailable here. Calibration margins do not adjudicate the hidden outcome. **Ineligible.** |
| Tudball et al., *Sample-constrained partial identification with application to selection bias*, Biometrika 2023. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/asac042) · [UK Biobank access](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/about-us/how-we-work/access-to-uk-biobank-data/) | Paper CC BY 4.0. Participant records are application-controlled and normally platform-bound. [Access fees and analysis costs](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/use-our-data/fees/) are not per-policy acquisition costs. | Volunteer cohort, auxiliary population constraints and education/income example. Observed cohort outcomes do not label unseen units. | Closest applied literature example for sensitivity under selection. Public paper cannot provide independent adjudication of nonparticipants or the GENEVRA endpoint. **Methods comparator, no restricted download.** |
| Koh et al., *WILDS: A Benchmark of in-the-Wild Distribution Shifts*, ICML 2021, iWildCam. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/koh21a.html) · [Official loader and license](https://github.com/p-lambda/wilds/blob/main/wilds/datasets/iwildcam_dataset.py) | Loader lists dataset CDLA-Permissive-1.0. The archive is large; access health and reuse of particular source images were not tested here. | Camera trap images with site-disjoint evaluation and annotated species labels. Cameras and bursts induce dependence. Labeled held-out photos are a benchmark population, not a probability sample of unobserved landscapes. | Useful for selective classification under site shift. Missing design inclusion, independently verified unobserved sites and costs. Different estimand. **Ineligible.** |
| Barrick, Deatherage and Lenski, *A Test of the Repeatability of Measurements of Relative Fitness in the Long-Term Evolution Experiment with Escherichia coli*, 2019. [Dryad record](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.cz8w9ghzm) | Public archive route. No explicit asset license confirmed on the inspected dataset page; verify before redistribution. | Relative competition fitness for 12 established lineages across selected generations and two laboratories. Both laboratories measure the same lineages. Outcomes have measurement error. | Strong independent-measurement analogue. Lineages, not visits, are candidate independent units. No independently sampled mutation frame, no reference outcome for the GENEVRA score and no matched costs. **Ineligible.** |
| Notin et al., *ProteinGym: Large-Scale Benchmarks for Protein Fitness Prediction and Design*, NeurIPS 2023. [Original benchmark and citation](https://github.com/OATML-Markslab/ProteinGym) · [Data record](https://zenodo.org/records/15293562) | Repository code MIT. Check the versioned dataset record and assay-specific upstream rights separately before use. No data redistributed. | Curated deep mutational scanning observations and separate clinical tasks. Related proteins, variants and assays create clustered measurement context. Fitness scores are assay-specific proxies. | Can benchmark a new protein-task estimand with assay-level holdout. Curated inclusion is not a target probability frame. It supplies no matched budget or qualified GENEVRA labels. **Ineligible.** |

Administrative access can add a source of identifying information while retaining its own omissions and measurement error. A related dataset does not substitute for an independently defined target or linked and adjudicated outcomes.
