# Phase 21 completion report

**Decision.** The public-data diagnostic is complete. No screened dataset supports a valid matched-cost external evaluation of the GENEVRA certificate. The defensible contribution remains a reproducible benchmark and negative methodological feasibility result. External and biological validation remain unestablished. No new synthetic study or field campaign ran.

## Scientific contribution and evidence

The central question is whether an omission-aware policy can issue more useful correct certificates at the same acquisition cost while controlling false certification against an independently enumerated target. The method combines established confidence bounds and partial identification. Its distinct contribution is the reproducible access-, loss- and cost-controlled stress design. Shared blindness is already explained by established selection and identification theory. Existing synthetic observations establish failure of a particular workflow under a planted law, not a general performance advantage. See the [literature and novelty matrix](LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY.md).

The Phase 18 shared-blind case reproduced zero coverage and 180 false certificates in 512 runs. The Phase 19 extension recovered coverage under its valid omission cap and lost covered precision under the allocation proxy. Both statements remain conditional on the frozen laws. The Census diagnostic is a separate observed-data exercise. The published March 2022 low, middle and high DA rates were −5.3%, −5.4% and −5.5%. Recalculation from the separate April 2024 national counts reproduced −5.459850%, rounding to the published −5.46%. This is **net coverage error**. It cannot supply the gross omitted mass, independently adjudicated GENEVRA labels or method-level certificate risk. A different-universe PES estimate is contextual only. ## External feasibility and next study

Eight candidate sources were assessed individually. The Census Bureau aggregate tables support a reference-sensitivity diagnostic. EPA's rivers-and-streams assessment provides the strongest design analogue for a prospective application, with probability sampling and standardized measurements. Public records reviewed did not establish a time-matched, separately governed evaluation frame linked to operational access, adjudicated outcomes and actual cost. No external policy comparison was executed. The next credible step is one partner and a registered 12-episode **feasibility** pilot, after verifying off-frame access and reference precision. The pilot cannot meet the confirmatory safety requirement. See the [dataset assessment](DATASET_ASSESSMENT.md), [external validation design](EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md), [matched comparison](FAIR_COMPARISON_PROTOCOL.md) and [finite roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
