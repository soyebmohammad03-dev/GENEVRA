# Integrated methodology and contribution

**Research question.** When can a bounded paired-score mean or an anchor certificate be transported to an evaluation population at fixed access and budget, and when must the method report unresolved uncertainty?

Within a declared synthetic law write mu=(1−p)c+pt+qd. Here p is tail mass, c and t are the conditional clipped-body and tail means, q is residual mass, and d its mean residual. This decomposition is elementary. Stratification can sharpen conditional means. If p, q or support are unknown, their uncertainty must also enter the interval. Independent calibration can estimate them only for its own sampling law. Frame provenance and agreement test different properties from representativeness. If both channels omit the same target information, the target law is not point identified from those channels.

The extension isolates the tail mechanism with fixed conditional means and no residual. With reachable tail rate r, inaccessible mass m and hidden tail z, p=(1−m)r+mz. Independently defensible bounds on m and z are identifying information. Confidence bounds for r and endpoint propagation yield an assumption-conditional envelope. With exact r and unrestricted z, an upper mass cap U gives a score-width floor (t−c)U. More reachable draws cannot reduce that component. At the frozen span 234.2 and width target .60, the cap must be at most 0.2562% even before sampling uncertainty.

Keep three endpoints separate. Mean coverage asks whether the confidence set contains the synthetic target mean. A threshold call asks whether the interval resolves the .60 decision boundary. A source-anchor certificate compares the whole plausible range with .30 allowable error from the anchor. Full physical support can cover while abstaining, and a false certificate can occur while the threshold direction is correct.

The defensible contribution is a reproducible, assumption-labeled workflow for finding the point where sampling precision gives way to identification and decision limits. A synthetic failure suite can diagnose a method's behavior under the stated laws. It cannot estimate how often those laws arise in practice.
