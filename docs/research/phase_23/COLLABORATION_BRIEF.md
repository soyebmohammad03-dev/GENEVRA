# Prospective collaboration brief

**Review material only. No collaboration or field access is in place.**

GENEVRA seeks to understand whether two monitoring workflows can agree while missing some of the same eligible stream visits. We propose comparing two decision policies using the same purchased information and testing their decisions against an independently built and audited reference. A reach-week means one eligible stream segment in one registered sampling window. The target is the fraction of eligible reach-weeks classified positive by a specified lab assay.

The custodian would enumerate eligible visits, run an independent probability area audit, draw the reference sample and hold the blind. Field and lab teams would record custody, failures and assay QC. The policy team would freeze its decisions before the evaluator accesses reference outcomes. The evaluator would check whether the comparison used the same acquired information and cost, report unresolved references and compute prespecified safety and usefulness endpoints. The prospective partner would define assay, geography, governance and cost feasibility with the research team before registration.

The first authorized field activity, if qualified, would be at most the proposed 12-episode operational pilot. It would assess access, auditability, blinding, adjudication and costs. Its precision cannot establish the 5% safety criterion. Prelaunch outputs include a permitted roster and sampling plan, assay QA, a cost ceiling, role segregation and a signed aggregate release plan.
