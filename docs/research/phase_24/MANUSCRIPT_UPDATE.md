# Phase 24 manuscript contribution

This note extends the [Phase 23 manuscript foundation](../phase_23/MANUSCRIPT_FOUNDATION.md). It preserves the earlier conclusions and frozen results.

**Proposed contribution.** A versioned research artifact reproduces known-truth failures of matched-access certification and measures recovery when valid omission information is supplied. It combines fixed scenario laws, separately reconstructed intervals, a complete per-replicate submission schema and independent scoring of multiple decision endpoints. Existing partial-identification, survey sampling, bounded-confidence and abstention methods underlie these calculations. See the [literature and novelty audit](../phase_23/LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY.md).

**Results.** In the Phase 18 shared-blind case, zero of 512 intervals covered the target and 180 of 512 runs issued false certificates. All 180 issued certificates in that case were false, with a pointwise Wilson interval of 97.91% to 100% among issued certificates. Coverage among decisive calls was 0/512 and coverage among certificates was 0/180. No threshold label was wrong. The simultaneous one-sided upper bound for unconditional false-certification risk was 40.73%. Three prespecified paired utility comparisons include zero. In Phase 19, a valid cap restored large-omission coverage to 512/512, with no precise intervals. The small-omission matched-budget comparison gave 10/512 covered and precise for the planned allocation versus 450/512 for equal spend. Bad cap and hidden-support controls both produced zero coverage.

**Independent implementation, internal execution.** The archived verifiers reconstruct numerical estimates from frozen counts, including separate likelihood inversion and coverage hull implementations. The new scorer independently aggregates decisions and computes Wilson bounds and paired Hoeffding intervals. Its inputs are counts and verified replicate outputs. It shares the archive schema, numerical dependencies and scoring definitions with the benchmark. Existing final tables serve as comparison targets. No external researcher has run or evaluated the package.

**Limits and future work.** The scenarios use planted laws and stipulated acquisition costs. Correctly checked arithmetic cannot validate those laws or cap assumptions. The scoreable competition is exploratory because the target truth and prior outcomes are public. Baseline coverage should be expanded to a prespecified variance-aware allocator and closely related benchmarks under equal information and cost. Independent technical review, external artifact reuse and independently governed target/reference acquisition remain necessary for broader claims. The [Census reconstruction](../phase_21/REFERENCE_DIAGNOSTIC.md) supplies aggregate description only. The proposed field study remains separate future work.
