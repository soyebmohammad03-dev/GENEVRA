# Author actions before submission

- Confirm authors, order, contributions, affiliations and the corresponding author's public contact and ORCID where requested. The draft carries the known author name and leaves affiliation and correspondence pending.
- Confirm originality, prior dissemination and its disclosure, coauthor approval if applicable, and any concurrent submissions.
- Supply accurate funding, competing interests, acknowledgments and any required tool-use declarations under the journal's applicable policy.
- Confirm rights and compatibility for code, figures, sources and supplementary material. Review the final submission source inventory and journal license terms.
- Confirm archival version and availability links. Use an assigned persistent identifier only after it exists.
- Decide whether practical usefulness, empirical illustration where applicable, and independent reproduction evidence support a submission. Record actual evidence and any limitations.
- Verify portal fields and upload attachments immediately before submission. The prepared letter is an unsigned draft.
