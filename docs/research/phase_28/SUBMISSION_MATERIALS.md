## Reviewer-facing contribution

GENEVRA combines known-truth shared-frame stress cases with matched acquisitions and a separate implementation that verifies interval construction, costs, coverage, useful precision, certificates and paired contrasts. The work applies established statistical methods. Its experimental contribution is to connect frame access and cap validity to different observed failures under declared costs. Its software contribution is a pinned, publicly replayable audit trail. The unsuccessful width proxy is retained as a comparator.

## Limitations statement

The benchmark evaluates public synthetic cases with stipulated tariffs and supplied restrictions. Independent frame audits, empirical costs, external reproduction and representative outcomes are outstanding. Synthetic scenario frequencies are not deployment frequencies. The external review protocol and a withheld comparison are separate future work.
