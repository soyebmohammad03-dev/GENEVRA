# Venue assessment and submission decision

Checked against the publisher and agency guidance on 9 November 2026. These are candidate routes conditional on additional evidence. No submission has been made. Recheck policies, charges and license eligibility before any later submission.

| Venue and plausible article type | Scope and relevant precedent | Reproducibility, access and costs | Gate for GENEVRA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Journal of Statistical Software, software article | Reusable statistical software with a clear contribution against implementations of similar scope. *MCSim: A Monte Carlo Simulation Program* is a simulation-software precedent, rather than a direct benchmark comparator. [Article](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v002i09) | Standalone replication script, software and reproduction data/materials. Follow journal formatting and qualifying open-source licensing. Journal describes free submission and open access. DOAJ records no APC. [Author guidance](https://www.jstatsoft.org/authors) · [Submission guide](https://www.jstatsoft.org/guides/submission) · [DOAJ record](https://doaj.org/toc/1548-7660) | Conditional first software route. Demonstrate reuse by independent users, make the comparator against nearest software concrete, and confirm that the artifact qualifies as a sufficiently distinct statistical software contribution. |
| Survey Methodology, regular article or a substantially shorter scoped note | Survey data quality, integration and design. Lohr's multiple-frame review is a close substantive precedent. Review guidance values a new relevant insight or an adaptation solving a practical problem. Simulations can support evidence. [Scope](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/index-eng.htm) · [Lohr article](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/2021002/article/00008-eng.htm) | Public electronic journal. Follow manuscript, equation, data and table formatting guidance. The reviewed guidance did not specify an APC, so no fee commitment is assumed. [Submission](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/manuscripts-eng.htm) · [Reviewer guidance](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/12-001-x/reviewer-examinateur-eng.htm) | Best survey-science fit if an independently reviewed case shows why the failure changes a realistic sampling decision. Shared blindness as a known identification issue is insufficient on its own. |
| Journal of Survey Statistics and Methodology, statistical or methodological article | Survey and census designs, coverage and nonresponse. *The Effects of Sampling Frame Designs on Nonresponse and Coverage Error* compares frame choices using Dutch survey data. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1093/jssam/smy016) | Requires availability statements and TOP Level 1 disclosures. Complex survey analyses may require PRICSSA. Standard and optional paid OA licenses are available. Current exact OA charge and institutional eligibility were not verified. [Author instructions](https://academic.oup.com/jssam/pages/General_Instructions) | Consider after independent practical validation or a convincing frame-design application. The frozen simulation is no substitute for field costs, nonresponse dispositions or reference measurements. |

**Current decision.** Keep submission on hold. The strongest route is a benchmark and verification paper once independent readers can reproduce it and establish added value relative to the closest software. A scoped negative-feasibility technical report is the fallback. Empirical or new-method routes require separate evidence.
